### What I'm here for

I'm at **SmallSat 2026** presenting *F Prime to Flight Faster* — hardware-in-the-loop
continuous integration for CubeSat development, with [Saidi Adams](https://www.linkedin.com/in/saidi-adams/)
and [Michael Pham](https://www.linkedin.com/in/michael-pham-04743a161/).

Every commit to the PROVES flight software is deployed to and exercised on a real
engineering satellite before it can merge. Over the past year that's **1,917 commits
gated on hardware**, **132 hours of hardware test runtime**, and an **18-minute median
commit-to-verdict**. All the hardware and software is open source.

[**Read the poster →**](https://txst-space-lab.github.io/fprime-to-flight-faster/)
